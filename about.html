About DSM College Parbhani

Deogiri Shikshan Sanstha's College (DSM College), Parbhani is a premier educational institution dedicated to fostering academic brilliance and moral values. Located in the heart of Parbhani, Maharashtra, the college has a rich legacy of producing skilled graduates, scientists, professionals, and leaders.


Our campus offers state-of-the-art infrastructure, a rich library filled with thousands of reference books, dedicated research spaces, and an encouraging environment that drives student success in university exams and competitive fields.
